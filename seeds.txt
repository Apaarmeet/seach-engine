# Seed set chosen for LINK DENSITY, not just volume.
#
# Anchor text and PageRank are graph signals: they only carry information
# when pages in the corpus link to other pages in the corpus. A random
# scrape of unrelated domains produces a graph with no edges and therefore
# no usable authority signal — which is exactly what a flat Common Crawl
# slice gives you. These seeds sit in a densely interlinked cluster about
# India, so the link signals actually light up at small corpus size.
